# Aero logo exploration

This folder follows the installed **Logo Generator** skill workflow.

## Brand brief

- **Brand:** Aero
- **Product:** super-light YouTube player
- **Core concepts:** lightness, airflow, speed, focus, reduced clutter
- **Primary context:** dark UI, small app icon, website header
- **Collab context:** Aero × IVE fan edition
- **Direction:** minimal, geometric, scalable, restrained

## Concepts

1. **Slipstream A** — an A-frame plus a single airflow stroke. It keeps the brand initial while making motion the secondary idea.
2. **Negative Play** — a solid A with a play-shaped negative-space cut. Strongest direct connection to video playback.
3. **Airfoil Loop** — a single aerodynamic loop with a secondary slipstream. More abstract and premium.
4. **Flight Path** — two asymmetric motion curves and a terminal node. Least literal, most lightweight.
5. **Wing Lines** — a denser line system that combines A/wing/airflow while preserving enough structural weight for an icon.
6. **Orbit A** — a minimal A crossed by an orbital path with a single node. Suggests continuous playback and flow.

## Skill checklist

All concepts use:

- `viewBox="0 0 100 100"`
- `currentColor`
- 1–2 primary ideas
- substantial negative space
- primary stroke weights around 3–4
- no gradients, shadows, or decorative effects in the core mark

Open `index.html` to compare the six variants at large and favicon scale.
